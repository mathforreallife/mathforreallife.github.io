Privacy

Short version: no cookies, no ads, no accounts, no tracking that can identify you.

What this website counts

To know whether the website is useful, it counts a few anonymous events: a page being viewed, the bonus being unlocked, a bonus file being downloaded and the “Buy on Amazon” button being clicked. With each event it keeps the page, the website that linked here (only its name), any campaign tag in the link (such as ?src=pinterest), the country your network is in and whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer.

It never stores your IP address, a cookie, a device ID or anything else that could tell visits apart or identify you. The counts are kept on Cloudflare's servers and only the owner of this website can read them.

What stays on your device

Your choice of light or dark theme and, if you tick “Remember it on this device”, your bonus code. Both are kept in your browser's local storage, never sent anywhere, and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Other websites

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep technical logs to run and protect its service (see GitHub's privacy statement). To open the Amazon store of your country, the page asks the counting service which country your network is in; that answer isn't stored. The “Buy on Amazon” button takes you to Amazon, where Amazon's own privacy notice applies. Links to social networks take you to those networks.

Our publishing apps

“Math For Real Life Poster” is our own publishing tool, made and used only by Math For Real Life. It publishes the short videos and trick cards that we design ourselves to our own accounts: the Instagram account and Facebook Page @mathforreallife (through Meta's APIs) and the Pinterest account @mathforreallife (through the Pinterest API). It has no other users: nobody else can sign in to it, and it is not offered to the public.

What the apps can see and do

  • Pinterest: it signs in to our own Pinterest business account with Pinterest's official sign-in (OAuth), with permission to read our account's name, read and create our boards, and read and create our Pins. It creates one Pin per video on our board “Mental Math”, with the image, a title, a description and a link to the book, and it reads the Pins already on our account only to avoid pinning the same card twice.
  • Instagram and Facebook: it publishes our Reels to our own account and Page, adds a comment with the link to the book, and reads our own recent posts only to avoid posting the same video twice.
  • It never reads, collects, stores or shares data about other people: not their profiles, Pins, boards, followers, comments or messages.

Where its data is kept

The only things the apps keep are their access keys for our accounts and a list of which of our videos were posted where (the post's link and time). Both are kept in a private, access-controlled repository that only the owner of Math For Real Life can open, and are used only to publish our posts. Nothing is sold or given to anyone. To stop the apps, we revoke their access in the platform's settings; their keys then stop working and are deleted.

Deleting your data

This website and our apps keep no personal data about you, so there is nothing to delete. If you think we hold something about you anyway, write to mathforreallife@proton.me or send a message to @mathforreallife on Instagram or Facebook, and we will delete it within 30 days.
